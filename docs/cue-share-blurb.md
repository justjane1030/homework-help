# Cue — class-chat share blurb (updated Nov 12, 2026)

App name chosen Nov 12, 2026: **Cue** — tagline: "homework help, in your own voice."
Updated from Jane's Nov 12 blurb draft (source: chat 2026-11-12): only the name/tagline changed; all other wording is hers as approved. Post the demo video and screenshot first, then paste the blurb under them.

## Long version

Hi everyone 💛 I'm Jane, mom of two boys (6 and 8). I built a little app called **Cue** — homework help, in your own voice — and I wanted to share it with you.

📸 Snap a photo of tonight's homework sheet, and it tells *you* exactly what to say to coach your child through it. The answer stays hidden — the kids still do the thinking.

It's not a chatbot handing out answers to copy. It's the school's method, translated into parent words.

🎥 I attached a screenshot and a short video of me using it, so you can see how it works before you try.

It's free, and it doesn't save your photos. And honestly? I hope you don't need it — if your child is happily independent with homework, I'm genuinely glad for you. But on the nights when it isn't easy, it's here:

https://homework-captain.netlify.app/

I'm still testing it, so if anything looks off, please tell me. And if it helps your evening, pass it to one more parent 💛
