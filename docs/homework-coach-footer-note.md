# Cue (formerly Homework Coach) — in-app footer note (final, Nov 12, 2026)

App name chosen Nov 12, 2026: **Cue** — tagline: "homework help, in your own voice."
Final opening line chosen Nov 12, 2026: "…not because the kids can't do it, but because helping is the hard part: two kids bent over their homework, one little one in my lap, and not quite enough of me to go around."

I built this as a parent, with a lot of love (and a few late nights), to make homework time a little easier — not because the kids can't do it, but because helping is the hard part: two kids bent over their homework, one little one in my lap, and not quite enough of me to go around.

🔒 **Privacy, parent to parent:** I don't save your photos — there's no drawer on my side where they go. When you snap, the photo takes a quick trip to Google's Gemini AI so it can read the worksheet, and the only thing I ever count is how many scans happen, so I know the app is working. The honest part: this runs on Google's free tier, and Google may use what gets submitted to make its AI better — a Google reviewer could even see it. So do me one favor: crop out your child's name before you snap, and keep report cards and IEPs for the teacher, not the app.

⭐ **Keep it handy:** bookmark this page, or add it to your Home Screen. It works on any phone or browser.

💬 **Ideas or problems?** I'd love your feedback!

I hope you don't need this app — if your child is happily independent with homework, I'm genuinely glad for you. But on the nights when it isn't easy, it's here.

💛 **If it helped, pass it to one more parent.** That's the best thank-you.

*The parent-to-parent print (not small — just honest): grown-ups, please do the snapping — this is a tool for helping your child with their homework, not a kids' app for little hands alone. I'm a mom, not a teacher, and this has no connection to any school. AI is clever, but it can misread a worksheet (pencil is tricky!) — if a hint here ever disagrees with your child's teacher, the teacher wins, every time. And these are helping hints, not grades or a judgment of your child; if homework is a battle most nights, that's a chat with the teacher, not an app fix. 💛*
