Snap tonight's homework 📷

Snap your child's homework and get a short, warm guide that tells you exactly what to say.

  1. Scan one worksheet, or pick several pages at once.
  2. Coach with the tips. Answers stay hidden until you tap.
  3. Play the Quick Check game made from that homework, to see if your child really learned it. It's optional. Tap Scan another to skip it.
Reading the worksheet…
Tip: good light, fill the frame with the page, hold steady. Printed sheets read reliably; pencil handwriting is harder.

A note from Jane
I made this to make homework time a little easier. It's free, personal and not a business.
🔒 Your privacy: this app doesn't save your photos, your child's work or any personal info. Each photo is sent to Google's Gemini AI to be read, then the app forgets it. The only thing kept is a count of scans per family code, so I know the app is working. Please crop out your child's name before you snap.
⭐ Keep it handy: bookmark this page, or add it to your Home Screen. It works on any phone or browser.
💬 Ideas or problems? Feel free to send me any feedback!
