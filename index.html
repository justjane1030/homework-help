Snap tonight's homework

Snap your child's homework and get a short, warm guide that tells you exactly what to say.

  1. 1Scan one worksheet, or pick several pages at once.
  2. 2Coach with the tips. Answers stay hidden until you tap.
  3. 3Play the Quick Check game made from that homework, to see if your child really learned it. It's optional. Tap Scan another worksheet to skip it.

A note from Jane 💛
I built this as a parent, with a lot of love and a few late nights. Not because kids can't do homework, but because helping is the hard part: two kids bent over their work, one little one in my lap, and not quite enough of me to go around.
🔒 Privacy: I don't save your photos. Google's Gemini AI reads each one, and on its free tier Google may use or review what's sent. I only count scans, using a random ID on your phone, never your name or email. Please crop out your child's name, and keep report cards and IEPs out of the app.
📌 Bookmark it or add it to your Home Screen. If it helped, pass it to one more parent. 💬 Feedback welcome!
Just me: This is a personal project from one parent. I'm not affiliated with any school, district, company or organization. The AI can get things wrong sometimes, so trust your judgment and your child's teacher.
For grades K–5. Follows Florida B.E.S.T. standards for reading and math, and Florida science standards. Answers are hidden behind a tap so the child does the thinking.
